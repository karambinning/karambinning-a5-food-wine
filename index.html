01 — On the shelves

The shop in a sentence:
everyday essentials, with the wine taken seriously.

Wines

The everyday bottle and the occasional one. Old-world, new-world, and a small house selection chosen by the counter.

Spirits

The names you’d expect on a back-bar shelf, plus a working selection of mixers kept cold next to them.

Beers & Ales

British lagers, continental imports, and craft cans as space allows. Singles, four-packs and multipacks.

Groceries

Bread, milk, tinned goods, snacks, cereals and the small things you ran out of on the way home.

Tobacco

Cigarettes, rolling tobacco, papers, filters and lighters — kept behind the counter, served on request.

News & Sweets

The daily papers, magazines, confectionery, ice cream and soft drinks. The corner-shop staples done properly.
